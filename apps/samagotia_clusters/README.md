# Samagotia Clusters

Standalone BMW iDrive 7 app with one F8X cluster design. Its F8X runtime, settings page, vehicle decoder and artwork are independent of the sample Cluster Themes app. It uses the credited MGU App Kit infrastructure and requires the separately installed `cluster-render` 0.5.3 or later.

Build from the studio root with `python3 tools/build_clusters.py`. Use `--stage-only` for the Mac preview stage. A normal build uses an isolated temporary stage and creates a Store package plus dependency catalog. No command here deploys to a vehicle.

The parked-test build starts with the BMW cluster selected, disables simulated data on the car, and returns to the BMW cluster if speed or RPM is missing or stale. Renderer disconnection also requires manual reselection after recovery. Live RPM and oil-temperature field decoding are implemented, but have not been tested on a G82. The oil scale stays fixed at 70–170 °C (or its Fahrenheit counterpart). RPM limit source defaults to Auto (BMW live), using `de.bmw.infrastructure.VehicleInformation.DataPowerTrain2CRC.rPMEngineMaximumAllowed` notifications and the mapped 50 RPM/count decode. The field and byte encoding are source-mapped; availability, physical scaling and cold-redline tracking still need confirmation on each car, including the G82. The subscription requests notifications only, with no invented getter. A conservative three-second freshness window is enforced; the SOME/IP delivery rate itself has not been measured on the G82. Missing, invalid, stale or disconnected limit data hides the entire warning band and disables shift-light building/flashing. A configured redline is used only after explicitly selecting Manual. The moving oil-based warmup estimate is SIM ONLY and never substitutes for BMW limit data on the car. Parking-brake status has a mapped live API, without a G82 capture confirming it is populated. Full-pedal and optional vehicle fields still require verification on each car.

Settings shows the limit source, decoded RPM, raw byte, freshness and provider state for the parked check. Older firmware without the `DataPowerTrain2CRC` catalog field may require installer compatibility handling before this build can install.

The visible options are F8X design, speed/distance units, temperature units, digital speed, gear number in D, backlight colour (Original or Amber), information page, bottom row, date format, clock, time zone, fuel tank size, RPM limit source, explicit manual redline, and shift-point offset. Hidden `demo` and `coldGuard` fields exist only for the separate Mac test bench; no simulator ships in this app.

Cruise indication reads only `DisplayDataServer2.functionalLibraryData`: the icon beside the left turn indicator is green when active and orange in standby. Its speedometer marker uses the same curve as the needle, converting an explicitly supplied MPH set speed to km/h first. Unsupported modes, stale data and provider loss hide both; a missing speed unit hides the marker. No guessed unit or legacy signal fallback is used. This is source-mapped integration; Server2 delivery and live MPH accuracy have not been tested on a G82.

Day needle cast shadows use separate shaft-shaped textures with a short soft edge and fixed screen-space lighting offsets. The shadows rotate with the smoothed needles; blade overlap changes their visible side naturally. Night retains its original shadow textures and lighting.

Original copyrights and licences are retained in `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`.

The RPM mapping is documented in the [public BMW M8 i-Level 20-07 catalog](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/34052657/someip-interfaces.json) and corroborated against the [F-series CAN maximum-RPM definition](https://github.com/billyjack2/CAN-DBC-Collection/blob/6e2f88fd6cd9c1f47d0db7bd022711b106f27027/BMW/F_Series/can1.dbc). The catalog establishes the field, not per-vehicle delivery or cold-limit behavior.

Backlight colour is a local visual preference, independent of BMW signals. Original is the default and retains the saved artwork. Amber warms only numbers, ticks, scale rails, unit labels and the fuel/oil symbols in Day and Night. Metal, hub lighting, red needles, warning colours and information text retain their original finish. The setting replaces four resident face images in place; motion performs no colour conversion or texture upload.

The plain lower tachometer arc completes the open ring beneath the gear indicator. It is a static native effect aligned to the actual tachometer pivot and scale, with grey Day/Night colours and no extra image allocation or vehicle-state inference. Day rims use a restrained continuous directional steel finish across their thicker sections; Night metal artwork is unchanged.

Fuel reserve indication reads `KombiIF.statusRangeTankStatus.statusFluidLevelFuelTankSpare` from the existing live stream. Fresh `AKTIV`/1 shows the message, pump icon and amber triangle; fresh `NICHT_AKTIV`/0 clears them. If that flag is absent/invalid, valid live range at or below 50 km triggers the warning, regardless of display units. Provider loss or a sample age of 25 seconds makes the warning unknown and hides it; reconnect requires a fresh sample. No fuel-percentage guess is used. The triangle currently represents this implemented fuel warning, not every BMW Check Control fault. The catalog signal is verified; G82 delivery remains untested.


## Information pages (0.15.0)

Selectable pages are Off, Outside temperature, Date, Average fuel consumption, Range remaining, Time and Average speed. With digital speed off, outside temperature and time stay in the two fixed rows; duplicate selectable entries are suppressed. With digital speed on, Time and Outside temperature can be selected individually. The custom speed + clock + consumption arrangement is retained. Fresh fuel-reserve warnings temporarily select numeric range without changing the saved page; clearing/expiring the warning restores it. This custom priority uses the same independently freshness-checked warning as the existing message and triangle.

Temperature, distance, 12/24-hour clock, date order/separator and fuel economy default to Follow car. Explicit preferences override them. `Vehicle.statusNewCurrentUnitsStatusExt` is polled every ten seconds; missing preferences use metric, 24-hour and D.M.Y styling where needed, with a settings notice. Fuel economy uses the packet's explicit unit if the car preference is unavailable; UK and US gallons are never conflated. Exterior Celsius preserves half-degree resolution, and odometer/trip formatting retains leading zeroes.

Time/date defaults to `KombiIF.statusShowTimeDate`, polled every second. Car-local components are displayed directly, without applying the Mac/head-unit timezone again. Missing or invalid time shows `--`; a supported clock with unknown date-year encoding can still show valid time. Full literal calendar years are accepted; offset/two-digit years are rejected rather than guessing a century. The catalog supplies date order and separator, **not year width**; automatic F8X dates use two-digit years. System clock is an explicit alternative with its own timezone setting. None of these new readers has been tested on the G82.

Range, fuel litres, odometer and average readings expire after 25 seconds; exterior temperature after five seconds. Invalid fields and provider loss clear independently. Average speed/consumption use the verified 0.1 scale and 4095 invalid sentinel, including numeric or named unit enums. Stationary valid zeroes remain readings, not missing data.

The independently resettable factory trip counter is **not verified**. `wayPassed` is a travel-computer value, so its unused production subscription has been removed; it is not shown as the factory trip. On the car the trip slot shows `--`; the Mac supplies an explicitly simulated separate trip fixture for layout review. BC stalk input, current-consumption physical scaling, and a complete cross-car SLI decoder remain unverified and are not connected. No trip-reset command is sent. Navigation/Waze remains deferred. Evidence and remaining validation are in `docs/F8X-info-page-signal-gaps.md` in the source project.


## Door, bonnet and boot display (0.16.0)

The lower-right warning area now supports a small original top-down silver car with amber open side doors and red bonnet/boot sections. The message sits beneath the tachometer arc; gear, main dials and central BC readouts remain in place. Two-/four-door silhouettes are selected by a conservative model/body mapping from `Vehicle2.statusVehiclePartType`. Vehicle settings provides Automatic, 2 doors or 4 doors and Automatic/LHD/RHD steering overrides. These are display policies, not a wire-provided door count or exact model-specific 3D artwork. An SUV uses the four-door symbolic profile. Physical left/right requires known steering side or an explicit override; unknown side data produces generic text without an invented closed-car diagram.

`DoorsWindowsRoofMirrors.CentralLockingSystemAndPanelStatus` and `CentralLockingSystemAndPanelStatus2` are notification-only. Exact contact enums map to open, closed or unknown; no getter, lock/unlock command or short expiry timer is invented. States belong to the provider session, and provider loss/reconnect clears contacts until another sample. The first meaningful Status2 packet owns the whole snapshot for that session; an initial all-invalid Status2 cannot hide valid legacy data. Metadata uses a real getter every ten seconds and expires independently after sixty seconds. Delivery cadence and physical side semantics still need checking on the G82. Contact states are binary, not measured door angles. A secondary boot contact produces a generic tailgate warning; no upper/lower split-tailgate meaning is assumed.

The shared presenter uses app precedence Hood open, Boot open, Tailgate open, Door(s) open, then Fuel reserve. All confirmed drawable openings remain highlighted even when another closure owns the text. Only the referenced hood warning uses red text; side-door and boot labels are pale. Closing doors restores a still-active fuel message and leaves its attention triangle visible. Numeric low-fuel range priority remains independent. These rules are not claimed to duplicate BMW Check Control arbitration or hide timers. No all-closed green reassurance is shown from missing data.

The diagram is one resident 72×116 RGBA image, counted twice by the renderer (~0.064 MiB). Its bounded cache and state key avoid re-rasterizing or uploading it while unchanged; needle animation remains independent. Vehicle settings update the diagram without rebuilding needle layers. The default layout has about 30 layers and 8.6 MiB image memory.

BMW door/Check Control popups may take the panel slot. The app changes no native ownership, yielding or takeover flags; it must not fight BMW to keep its replica visible. The Mac proves our decoding, priority, pixels and fallback logic, not G82 reception or native warning visibility. No car has been connected for this feature.

Mac controls are under **Simulated signals → Doors / panels (SIM)**: G82/G80/G05/unknown, steering side, provider availability and every closure. These feed car-shaped packets into the same reader as production. Test examples: a passenger front door in G82/LHD, the same input in RHD, G80 with both rear doors, bonnet plus boot, unknown steering, and provider loss/recovery while a warning is active. The Mac alone also supplies the explicitly simulated mileage trip fixture described above.
